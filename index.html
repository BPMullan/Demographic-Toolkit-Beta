Development Framework and Working Prototype

Brendan Mullan, Ph.D.

The Demographic Toolkit© is being developed as a systematic, accessible national demographic intelligence infrastructure integrating key demographic indicators, analysis, visualization, and geographic comparison. The materials below present the current development framework, the functioning EL613 beta prototype, and an example of detailed local demographic analysis.

Working Prototype

EL613 Demographic Toolkit Beta Prototype

Current Beta Prototype

EL613 is the current functioning beta prototype of the Demographic Toolkit©. It demonstrates the demographic data, indicators, analytical organization, visualization, and regional demographic capabilities initially developed for Thessalia, Magnesia and Pelion and then expanded nationally, regionally, and locally (where data is available).
